# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Policy — Sample Knowledge Document

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors entering industrial zones A, B, and C.

## Required PPE
- **Zone A (assembly floor):** ANSI-rated hard hat, safety glasses, and closed-toe shoes. High-visibility vest when forklifts are operating.
- **Zone B (chemical handling):** Hard hat, splash goggles, chemical-resistant gloves, and apron.
- **Zone C (warehouse):** Safety shoes with steel toe, high-visibility vest. Hard hat required near racking above 8 feet.

## Helmet / hard hat rules
- Hard hats must be worn in Zone A at all times.
- Hats with cracks, missing suspension, or paint covering manufacturer labels must be replaced.
- Do not wear baseball caps under hard hats.

## Incident reporting
Report missing or damaged PPE to the shift supervisor before starting work. Do not enter a zone without the required equipment.
